Questions

Before you apply

How much do I actually earn?

A flat 25% commission on every course and on the Certified Lean Practitioner Programme, every time someone enrols through your link.

How long does my link stay credited to me?

90 days from the moment someone clicks it — one of the longer windows going, because most people don't buy the first time they hear about something.

Do the people I refer get anything?

Yes. Every ambassador link carries a discount for whoever clicks it, so your recommendation does some of the selling for you.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts run monthly via PayPal, for sales made at least 30 days earlier. Once your lifetime earnings pass $100, you'll be asked to file a quick tax form before payouts continue.

Do I need a huge following to apply?

No. This is judged on genuine fit and intent to promote — not follower count.

Is there any cost to join?

None. Applying and staying an ambassador is free.
